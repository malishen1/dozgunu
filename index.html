Shot Day (Doz Günü)

A simple companion for people on weekly GLP-1 injections: shot-day reminders with injection-site rotation, protein, water and fiber goals, a weight chart, a side-effect diary and a one-tap summary for your doctor. Turkish, English and Spanish.

Shot Day is a tracking tool, not medical advice. Your doctor sets your dose and treatment.
